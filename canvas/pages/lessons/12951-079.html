  Learning Intention and Success Criteria

Learning Intention - Students will:

  • learn to answer questions that require the student to drag options into a specified order.

Success Criteria - Students can:

  • Use the online exam interface to order items.
  • Apply their knowledge to determine an appropriate order.

  Content

How to Answer "Drag into Order" Questions

Procedure:

  1. Read the question carefully.
  2. Analyse any stimulus to determine the ordering criteria.
  3. Move items up and down based on your criteria until the list is ordered.
  4. Re-read the question and make sure your answer is correct.

 

ACTIVITY: Practice HSC Style Question

Sample Answer

Question: Place the following MLOps steps in the correct order by clicking and holding each step and dragging it into its position.

Step 1. Read the question carefully

When reading the question, it's important to ask these two questions:

  1. What is the topic/content area? This question is testing our knowledge of the MLOps process. Revise 3.1 Machine Learning and Automation, if required.
  2. What are we required to do? Here we are being asked to drag each step into it's position, so we need to recall the MLOps process and apply it to order the steps presented here. Note, some of the steps may be combined or omitted, your job is to just make sure the steps presented are moved into a logical order that abides my the MLOps process.

There is no stimulus for this question, so we will skip step 2.

Step 3. Begin the sorting.

  1. Crude Ordering. Read each option and if you know it's position, move it directly there. E.g if you read the first option and realised it's near the end, just drag it to the end and vice versa.
  2. Fine Ordering. After you've moved the steps into roughly the right order, read each option again and refine the ordering until you are done.

Step 4. Double check answer

Before moving on, it's a good idea to scan over your answer one last time to ensure everything is in the right order.

 Marking Criteria (through the eyes of a marker)


 How do I get full marks?

  • There isn't too much to break down here, to get full marks you need to sort the list correctly, there are no partial marks. If you find this question tricky you should revise 3.1 Machine Learning and Automation.

 I'm completely stuck. How do I maximise my marks?

  • Give the question a go even if it's a guess. After this, flag the question so you can come back to it after you finish the rest of the exam, hopefully with a new perspective or idea on how to answer the question.
