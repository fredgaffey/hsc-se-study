  Learning Intention and Success Criteria

Learning Intention - Students will:

  • Learn to complete online exams for the HSC course.
  • Learn the most precise and most effective ways to answer all the different questions types.
  • Learn how put yourself in the shoes of a HSC marker so you self-mark and evaluate your practice responses.
  • Learn how to study effectively and prepare for exams from an academic and lifestyle perspective.

Success Criteria - Students can:

  • Navigate the NESA Online HSC exam platform.
  • Answer all the different questions types accurately and efficiency.
  • Prepare effectively for exams.
  • Complete questions through the eyes of a marker to self evaluate answers.
