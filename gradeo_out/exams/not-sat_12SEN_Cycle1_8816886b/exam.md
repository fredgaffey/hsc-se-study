## 3. End User Influence (SA, TKS 2025)

*Source: TKS 2025*

**Stimulus:**

A mobile payment app is used by both tech-savvy professionals and elderly users with limited digital experience. The company is redesigning the app's security features after discovering that many users disabled them due to confusion.

#### Part A — –/3 marks  `SHORT`

Describe two secure design solutions the developers could implement to ensure that users of varying experience levels can confidently use the app without compromising its security.

**Your answer:**

_(not answered)_

**Marking criteria:**
- [3] Describes two relevant and secure design solutions appropriate for users with varying experience levels

Clearly addresses both usability and security in context
- [2] Describes one relevant and secure design solution

OR

Describes two solutions with limited detail or relevance
- [1] Shows some understanding of usability or security in app design

**Sample answer:**

To improve usability without reducing security, developers could introduce simplified onboarding that walks users through setting up essential security features with visuals and plain language. This would help less experienced users avoid disabling protections out of confusion. Additionally, enabling adaptive security—where the app quietly increases protection (e.g., requesting verification for large sum transfers)—ensures advanced users are not frustrated by constant prompts, while still maintaining safety. These strategies ensure that all users can operate the app securely and with confidence.
