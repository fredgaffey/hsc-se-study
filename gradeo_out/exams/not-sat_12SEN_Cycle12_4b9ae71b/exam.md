**Sample answer:**

Activation functions play a critical role in neural networks by introducing non-linearity into the model. This non-linearity is essential because it allows the network to learn and model complex relationships between the input data and the outputs. Without activation functions, no matter how many layers a neural network has, the composition of linear transformations would remain linear. Common activation functions, like the Rectified Linear Unit (ReLU) and the sigmoid function, offer different properties. The ReLU function, for example, is computationally efficient and helps mitigate issues such as vanishing gradients in deep networks. By transforming the weighted sum of inputs into activations that can capture complex patterns, activation functions enable neural networks to perform tasks that linear models cannot, such as image and speech recognition.

**Sample answer:**

The environmental implications of increased automation include potential increases in energy consumption and ecological impacts due to the production and disposal of automated technologies. Although automation can boost production efficiency and reduce waste, the energy required for operation and the life cycle of the technologies must be managed sustainably. Economically, while automation drives productivity and growth, it also risks exacerbating wealth inequality by predominantly benefiting capital owners and highly skilled workers. This unequal distribution of wealth challenges sustainable development goals, which include reducing inequality and promoting inclusive economic growth. To counteract these effects, policies focusing on wealth redistribution, environmental regulations, and promoting equal access to education and training in new technologies are necessary to ensure a fair and sustainable future.
