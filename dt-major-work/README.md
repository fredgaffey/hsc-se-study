# Design & Technology major work: folio (SHAPE nomination)

My Year 12 Design & Technology major work folio, **nominated for SHAPE** (NESA's exhibition of outstanding HSC Design & Technology, Industrial Technology and Textiles major works).

The project is **L Flip**, the same project as my Software Engineering major project: a learner-driver logbook app plus smart L-plates that flip automatically. The D&T side covers the physical plates and hardware: research and user data, legislation, materials, the actuator and mechanism, CAD in Onshape, the network design, testing, and the evaluation.

- **[Folio - L Flip (final, submitted).pdf](Folio%20-%20L%20Flip%20(final,%20submitted).pdf)**: the submitted folio, 68 pages (27 Aug): proposal, research, finance and action plans, prototypes and ongoing evaluation, development and realisation, and the full Project Evaluation (functional, aesthetic, impact on the individual and on society).
- **[Project video.mp4](Project%20video.mp4)**: the 5-minute project video (re-encoded from the submitted `.mov` to fit GitHub; same content).
- The software side: [`../major-project/`](../major-project) (documentation, logbook, walkthrough video) and the code in [`../lflip/`](../lflip).

`archive/` has two earlier drafts (21 and 23 Aug) and an InDesign source export. They are incomplete; use the final PDF above.
