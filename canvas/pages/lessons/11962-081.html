  Content

How to Answer Multi-Choice Questions

Procedure:

  1. Read the question carefully.
  2. Analyse any provided stimulus.
  3. Read and eliminate options that cannot be the answer.
  4. Choose the most correct answer (or guess if required) from the remaining options.
  5. Re-read the question and make sure your answer is correct.

Question Breakdown & Tips and Tricks

Question: Which of the following scenarios best demonstrates confidentiality in software design? (1 mark)

Step 1. Read the Question Carefully

You need to dissect the question carefully and identify the following:

  1. What is the topic/content area? The content area is confidentially. This is located under the "Explore fundamental software design security concepts when developing programming code" dot point of the "developing secure code" subtopic of your syllabus. Revise 1.6 Security by Design, if needed.
  2. What are we required to do? Since this is a multiple choice question, we are required to select 1 of the four options. In this case, the question specifies we choose the scenario that best demonstrates confidentiality in software design.

This question doesn't have any provided stimulus, so we skip step 2.

Step 3. Read and eliminate answers

Read each option from the answers and eliminate the ones which cannot possibly be the answer.

 

A) Using hashing algorithms to verify the integrity of transmitted data.

Step 3.1 — Assess Option A (Eliminate!)

This option is somewhat related to securing data since hashing is often used to verify passwords, however the phrase "verify integrity of transmitted data" is more about data integrity than confidentiality.

 

B) Encrypting sensitive information so only authorised users can access it.

Step 3.2 — Assess Option B (Looks correct!)

Option B has lots of phrases that we associate with confidentiality including encrypting sensitive information, and ensuring only authorised people can access data.

 

C) Implementing digital signatures to prevent denial of actions by involved parties.

Step 3.3 — Assess Option C (Eliminate!)

This option is trying to be correct, but doesn't quite fit confidentiality. Digital signatures are more about non-repudiation which is ensuring both parties have peace of mind sending and receiving messages since they can verify authenticity.

 

D) Creating redundant systems to ensure availability during unexpected downtime.

Step 3.4 — Assess Option D (Eliminate!)

This option is completely wrong since availability during downtime has nothing to do with confidentiality.

 

Step 4 — Choose the Best Option

Since we've eliminated options A, C and D, that leaves us with B as the correct answer. If you have a question where you are tossing between two possibly correct answers, try and find something in one of them that doesn't quite fit your understanding of the topic and then choose the most correct answer. Not every option will always be perfect, but you need to eliminate the option that have too many issues and pick the best option from the four.

Step 5 — Double check your answer

If you're in a hurry, you can skip this step, but it's often worth taking 5-10 seconds to just reread the question before you move on and ensure the answer you've picked does indeed answer it. In this case, we can glance to make sure that the answer we have selected does indeed fit the question and that we haven't misread it.

Marking Criteria (from the perspective of a marker)

 

How do I get full marks?

Multi-choice questions are normally auto-marked, so the only way to get full marks is by selecting the correct option. If you struggle to answer this question, revise 1.6 Security by Design.

I'm complete stuck. How do I maximise my marks?

  • Don't leave the question unanswered. Even if you don't know the answer you should take an educated guess and then flag the question so you can come back to it later, hopefully with fresh eyes, to give it another go.
