Course overview

Course overview

This course has been structured for online and blended learning. Synchronous and asynchronous work has been developed, including discussions, activities and reflections. In person sessions will also be organised throughout the year to compliment the online course.

The Canvas course will also incorporate other interactive features such as quizzes, multimedia and recordings from a range of staff and experts in the relevant fields. 

Formative feedback will be embedded throughout the course to accompany the mandated summative assessment tasks that students will complete.

Please post and reply to the questions of others throughout the year. 

Expectations

The Year 12 course requires 120 indicative hours including both synchronous and asynchronous learning and working towards the completion of each assessment task.

The course modules have been 'chunked' into 2 week cycles. A cycle represents a typical school fortnight of 8 hours of class time for a subject. Each cycle therefore represents the amount of work you would be expected to complete during the 8 hours of class you would have sat face to face.

Year 12 Indicative Hours
Secure Software Architecture 30

You are expected to behave respectfully while online and face to face. You can email the course coordinator, mienna@kings.edu.au if you have any concerns throughout the year.

Learning Outcomes

  • SE-12-01 justifies methods used to plan, develop and engineer software solutions
  • SE-12-02 applies structural elements to develop programming code
  • SE-12-03 analyses how current hardware, software and emerging technologies influence the development of software engineering solutions
  • SE-12-04 evaluates practices to safely and securely collect, use and store data
  • SE-12-05 explains the social, ethical and legal implications of software engineering on the individual, society and the environment
  • SE-12-06 justifies the selection and use of tools and resources to design, develop, manage and evaluate software
  • SE-12-07 designs, develops and implements safe and secure programming solutions
  • SE-12-08 tests and evaluates language structures to refine code
  • SE-12-09 applies methods to manage and document the development of a software project

Assessment overview

Assessment Schedule

To access comprehensive information about assessment tasks and their respective evaluation standards, please navigate to the Assignments tab located on the left-hand side of the screen.

Assessment Task 1

Secure Software Architecture (15%)

Students will apply skills in computational thinking and programming to identify and resolve security issues in a given piece of software.

Due: Week 8 Term 4

Assessment Task 2

Topic Test (15%)

Students will complete an online challenge testing their theoretical knowledge and skills.

Due: Week 1-2 Term 2

Assessment Task 3

Software Engineering Project (40%)

Students will complete a Web Application project developing the front and back end. The task will be broken into two seperate sections.

Section 1: Due: Week 9 Term 1 (15%)

Section 2: Due: Week 1 Term 3 (25%)

Assessment Task 4

Trial Examination (30%)

Students will complete a online examination in the same style as the HSC.

Due: Term 3 assessment block

Marking

Students will be provided with a marking rubric for their assessment tasks throughout the year and marked as per the common grade scale for preliminary courses as designed by NESA.

NESA

Components and weightings for Year 12:

Performance band descriptors for Software Engineering

HSC performance band descriptions summarise the knowledge, understanding and skills typically demonstrated by students at each band level in a particular course. NESA uses these holistic descriptions to report a student's overall level of achievement in an HSC course.

Band 6

A student who typically performs at this level:

  • demonstrates comprehensive understanding of the influence of technologies on the development of software engineering solutions

  • demonstrates extensive knowledge and understanding of the social, ethical and legal implications of the application of software engineering solutions on the individual, society and the environment

  • applies sophisticated technical skills in the design and implementation of programming solutions

  • collects, uses, manages and stores complex data effectively, safely and securely

  • applies comprehensive skills in developing, managing and documenting software engineering projects

  • communicates logically and effectively using a range of terms, conventions and methods

Band 5

A student who typically performs at this level:

  • demonstrates broad understanding of the influence of technologies on the development of software engineering solutions

  • demonstrates thorough knowledge and understanding of the social, ethical and legal implications of the application of software engineering solutions on the individual, society and the environment

  • applies high-level technical skills in the design and implementation of programming solutions

  • collects, uses, manages and stores data effectively, safely and securely

  • demonstrates high-level skills in developing, managing and documenting software engineering projects

  • communicates logically, using appropriate terms, conventions and methods

Band 4

A student who typically performs at this level:

  • demonstrates sound understanding of the influence of technologies on the development of software engineering solutions

  • demonstrates sound knowledge and understanding of the social, ethical and legal implications of the application of software engineering solutions

  • applies sound technical skills in the design and implementation of programming solutions

  • collects, uses, manages and stores data safely and securely

  • demonstrates sound skills in developing, managing and documenting software engineering projects

  • communicates using relevant terms, conventions and methods

Band 3

A student who typically performs at this level:

  • demonstrates basic understanding of the influence of technologies on the development of software engineering solutions

  • demonstrates basic knowledge and understanding of the social and/or ethical and/or legal implications of the application of software engineering solutions

  • displays basic skills in programming

  • uses data safely and securely

  • displays basic skills in developing software engineering projects

  • communicates using basic terms and conventions and/or methods

Band 2

A student who typically performs at this level:

  • demonstrates limited understanding of the use of technologies

  • displays limited skills in programming

  • displays limited skills in developing software engineering projects

  • communicates with limited use of terms

Band 1

Performance at a lower level than Band 2 is reported as Band 1.
