## 2. Applications of Web Programming (SA, TKS 2025)

*Source: TKS 2025*

#### Part A — –/4 marks  `SHORT`

Compare e-commerce websites and Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) by describing two features of each and explaining one advantage each offers to online businesses.

**Your answer:**

_(not answered)_

**Marking criteria:**
- [4] Clearly compares two features of e-commerce sites and PWAs, with a well-explained advantage for each.
- [3] Describes features of both with some explanation of advantages, but lacks full detail or clarity.
- [2] Describes features or advantages for one type only or provides limited explanations.
- [1] Mentions features or advantages vaguely without clear comparison or explanation.

**Sample answer:**

E-commerce websites typically feature product listings and secure payment gateways. Product listings allow businesses to present detailed information and pricing, helping customers make informed decisions. Secure payment processing ensures customer data is protected during transactions, fostering trust and encouraging purchases. Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), on the other hand, offer offline access through caching and push notifications to keep users engaged with timely updates. The offline capability of PWAs enhances usability by allowing customers to browse content without an internet connection, while push notifications improve communication and drive repeat visits. For businesses, e-commerce sites streamline sales and order management, whereas PWAs enhance user experience and customer retention through their app-like functionality.
